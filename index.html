Profile

I run the strategy, brand and operations of a Michelin-acclaimed restaurant group, and I came up through every layer of the business to get here: kitchen, bar, floor, outlet management and hotel F&B leadership at Four Seasons and Mandarin Oriental.

My work sits between ownership and the teams on the floor. I turn investment goals into menus, service standards, budgets and people plans that hold up in daily service. I'm most useful where fine dining needs to grow without losing what made it worth a star.

I'm also hands-on with AI and digital tools, so the group can build in-house what it used to outsource.
